Getting There

By air

Fly into Regional International (RIA), about 40 minutes from the venue. Rental cars and rideshare are available at the terminal.

By car

Free on-site parking at The Orchard. The drive from downtown is a scenic 25 minutes along Route 9.

Getting around

We'll run a complimentary shuttle from The Orchard Inn and Sunrise Hotel to the venue and back on the wedding day.
