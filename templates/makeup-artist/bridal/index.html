From "yes" to "I do"

A clear path from the day you reserve to the moment you walk down the aisle.

  1. 6–12 months out

    Reserve your date

    A 30% retainer secures your wedding date. Saturdays May–October book first, so inquire early.

  2. 4–8 weeks out

    Bridal trial

    We design and photograph your look in real lighting, test options, and finalize every detail before the day.

  3. 1 week out

    Timeline lock

    I build a minute-by-minute getting-ready run-of-show around your photographer and ceremony start.

  4. Wedding morning

    On-location glam

    I arrive early, set up a clean station, and work the party in sequence — finishing the bride last for the freshest look.

  5. Before the ceremony

    Last-look check

    A final 15-minute touch-up so you walk down the aisle picture-perfect, with a kit handed off for the reception.
