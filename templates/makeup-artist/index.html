About Camille

Glam you can feel.
Art you can wear.

I grew up watching my mother get ready — the ritual of it, the transformation, the way the right look could change how she carried herself into a room. I became a makeup artist because I believe that feeling is for everyone.

I trained at NAIMIE's Beauty Supply in North Hollywood and went straight into commercial and editorial work, spending five years building a kit and a vision that could translate from a bridal suite to a film set to a fashion runway. I hold certifications in airbrush from Temptu and Dinair Pro, and I completed on-set training at Warner Bros.

What I bring to every booking is simple: full presence. I'm not rushing to my next client, I'm not distracted, I'm here to make you look — and more importantly feel — extraordinary.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know
before booking.

From trial timelines to travel policies — here's what brides and clients ask most.

  • A trial is strongly recommended and included in all bridal packages. It's an opportunity to test the look in real lighting, try different options, and ensure the final look photographs exactly how you envision. Most brides schedule their trial 4–8 weeks before the wedding date.
  • Wedding dates — especially Saturdays May through October — book out 6 to 12 months in advance. For editorial and commercial work, 3–4 weeks notice is typically sufficient. Special occasion bookings can often be accommodated within 1–2 weeks depending on calendar availability.
  • Yes. All bridal and event services are on-location — I come to your hotel, venue, or home. Travel within 30 miles of central Los Angeles is included. Beyond 30 miles, a travel fee of $1.50/mile (portal-to-portal) applies. For out-of-state weddings, travel and accommodation costs are covered by the client.
  • The bride's makeup typically takes 90 minutes. Bridesmaids and guests average 45–60 minutes each. When building your wedding morning timeline, I recommend scheduling the bride last, right before photography begins, to ensure the freshest possible look.
  • My kit includes professional-grade products from NARS, Charlotte Tilbury, MAC, Hourglass, and Armani Beauty. For sensitive or reactive skin, please let me know during booking — I carry fragrance-free, mineral, and hypoallergenic alternatives for most steps and can tailor the application to your skin's needs.
  • Yes. Airbrush foundation is available as an add-on ($45) or is included in select premium packages. Airbrush is ideal for long-wear events, photography, and humid weather. It delivers an ultra-lightweight, transfer-resistant finish. I use Temptu and Dinair Pro systems.
  • A 30% non-refundable retainer secures your date. The remaining balance is due 72 hours before your appointment. Cancellations with less than 72 hours notice forfeit the full balance. Wedding date changes can typically be accommodated with advance notice and subject to availability.
  • My specialty is makeup artistry. I work closely with a vetted network of bridal hairstylists in the LA area and am happy to coordinate referrals or co-book with a trusted stylist for your wedding day so you have a single seamless team.
◇

Travel note: Camille is based in Los Angeles and available for destination weddings and out-of-state shoots worldwide. International travel requires client coverage of flights, hotel, and ground transport plus the applicable day rate.
