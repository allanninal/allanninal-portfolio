What We Train

5 Disciplines. One Dojo.

    Art of 8 Limbs

BJJ Journey

The Belt Rank Progression

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu has one of the most rigorous and respected ranking systems in martial arts. Five belts — each a multi-year investment in technical mastery. There are no shortcuts.

White Belt
Blue Belt
Purple Belt
Brown Belt
Black Belt

  • White Belt

    0–2 years

    Survival, escapes, basic positioning, fundamental submissions

    "Survive a round without getting tapped by a blue belt."

  • Blue Belt

    2–4 years

    Guard retention, passing, transitions, mount control, 50+ techniques

    "Submit blue belts and survive against purple belts."

  • Purple Belt

    4–7 years

    Game development, advanced guard systems, competition strategy

    "Developing your own style and instructing lower belts."

  • Brown Belt

    7–10 years

    Mastery refinement, competition sharpening, teaching proficiency

    "Near-black-belt level — refining every detail of your game."

  • Black Belt

    10+ years

    Technical mastery, philosophy of the art, legacy and instruction

    "A lifetime achievement in the martial arts."

Promotion Policy

Belt promotions at Bushido Academy are awarded solely by Marcus Chen based on technical proficiency, mat time, competition experience, and attitude — never purchased or fast-tracked. Stripes are awarded between full belt promotions to mark progress. A white belt with 4 stripes is ready for blue belt consideration.

Do I need any martial arts experience to start?
None at all. Our Intro to Martial Arts course is specifically designed for complete beginners — it runs for 6 weeks and your first class is completely free. Most of our programs have explicit Beginner or All Levels sessions that assume zero prior experience. Show up, be respectful, and be willing to learn — that's all we ask.
What should I bring to my first class?
Comfortable athletic clothing (shorts and a t-shirt) and bare feet on the mat. We have loaner gi's (the traditional uniform) for BJJ and Karate if you want to try before you buy, and gloves are available at the front desk for Muay Thai. The most important thing you bring is your attitude.
How long does it take to get a BJJ black belt?
The BJJ journey from white to black belt typically takes 10–15 years of consistent training. Blue belt is usually 2–4 years, purple belt another 2–3, brown belt 1–3 more, and then the black belt grading itself. At Bushido Academy, belt promotions are awarded by Marcus based on your skill level, time at rank, and attitude on the mat — never by time alone. Many students find the belt rank progression is what motivates long-term commitment to the art.
What is the age range for Kids & Teens classes?
We run three separate age tracks: Little Dragons (ages 5–7), Juniors Martial Arts (ages 8–12), and Teens (ages 13–17). Each group has age-appropriate class structures, contact levels, and belt progression systems. Family classes run Sunday mornings with adults and youth on the mat together — a great way to get started as a family.
How is the MMA team different from regular classes?
The Competition MMA Team is a dedicated competitive program requiring intermediate skill in at least two disciplines (typically BJJ + Muay Thai). Team members follow a structured periodization plan with specific fight preparation phases. Tryouts are held quarterly and require a conversation with Marcus first. Regular classes are open to all — the MMA team is a separate application-based program for those targeting amateur or professional competition.
Can I train multiple disciplines on the same membership?
Yes. Our Monthly Unlimited plan ($149/month) gives you unlimited access to all disciplines — you can do BJJ in the morning, Muay Thai in the evening, and Judo on Saturday. Many students build a well-rounded game by cross-training disciplines simultaneously. Marcus or Priya will help you plan a training schedule that balances volume and recovery.
What safety protocols do you have for sparring?
Sparring is available for intermediate students and above and is always supervised by an instructor. All striking sparring requires full protective gear (headgear, mouthguard, shin guards, gloves, and groin protection). We run sparring at controlled intensity — ego-driven "going hard" is not tolerated and is grounds for removal from the session. BJJ rolling follows standard etiquette with tap-to-submit respected immediately. Safety is our non-negotiable.
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
Monthly memberships can be cancelled at any time with 30 days' written notice — we do not charge cancellation fees. Drop-in classes are non-refundable but can be rescheduled up to 4 hours before the class start. Private coaching sessions require 24 hours' notice for cancellation or rescheduling. Family plans are billed at the household level and can be adjusted if a family member needs to pause.
