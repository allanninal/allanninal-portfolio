Start Here · New Students

Your First Week is free

Free7 days · all disciplines

Every new student starts here. The First Week is seven full days of unlimited classes across every discipline — no payment information required, no commitment. It is designed so you can find the martial art that fits you before you ever pay a cent. Here is a suggested path through your free week.

  1. Days 1–2

    Foundations & Grappling

    Start with an Intro to Martial Arts or BJJ Fundamentals session — respect and etiquette, safe falling (ukemi), and your first grappling positions. No experience assumed; you set up the mat and learn to move.

  2. Days 3–4

    Striking & Movement

    Try Muay Thai Striking — shadowboxing warm-up, partner pad work, and heavy-bag rounds on the 8-limb system. Kids can slot into a Little Dragons or Juniors class the same week to feel the youth program.

  3. Days 5–7

    Pick Your Path

    Sample Judo, Karate, or a second BJJ class, then sit down with a coach to map a plan. By the end of the week you will know which discipline (or combination) fits — and which membership matches your rhythm.

Claim Your Free Week

First-time Bushido Academy students only, ages 5 and up. No payment details required to start — just book your first class. Loaner gis and gloves are available at the front desk. One free week per new student; the Intro to Martial Arts 6-week course continues where the free week leaves off.

Grading & Progression

How belt testing works at Bushido

Every discipline at Bushido Academy has a real, earned ranking system — belts are awarded by the head coach of the art based on technical proficiency, mat time, and attitude, never by time served alone or by payment. Below is how the grading calendar and curriculum progression work across the dojo.

How progression works

01

Stripes & Attendance Ongoing

Between full belt promotions, students earn stripes that mark progress within a rank. Stripes are awarded for attendance, technical improvement, and attitude — a white belt with four stripes is ready for blue-belt consideration.

02

Curriculum Checkpoints Per rank

Each rank has a defined syllabus — the positions, submissions, throws, kata, or combinations you are expected to demonstrate. Coaches track your checkpoints so you always know what stands between you and the next belt.

03

Formal Grading Days Twice per year

Karate kyu/dan gradings and Judo rank exams run on scheduled grading days each spring and fall. BJJ promotions are awarded at the coach's discretion year-round, often at open mats or after competition performances.

04

Competition & Black Belt Long-term

Competition experience accelerates progression and is encouraged but never required. The black belt — 10+ years in BJJ — is treated as a lifetime achievement, and the start of teaching the art to the next generation.

Grading fees (which cover the certificate, the new belt, and registration with the governing body where applicable) are billed separately from membership and only when you test. There is no fee to be considered for promotion. BJJ promotions follow the IBJJF framework, Karate follows the WKF kyu/dan system, and Judo follows the IJF ruleset. Kids grade on the Bushido Kids curriculum at their own pace.
