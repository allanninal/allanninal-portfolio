  • Seeded Rye

    Dense, dark, earthy. Caraway, sesame, and sunflower seeds folded through 80% rye flour. Exceptional toasted with butter.

  • Honey Oat Loaf

    $8

    Soft crumb, lightly sweet, oat-topped crust. Perfect for sandwiches or morning toast.

  • Jalapeño Cheddar Sourdough Sat & Sun only

  • Gluten-Free Sandwich Loaf Tue & Fri only — call ahead to reserve

    $12

    Rice flour and tapioca base. Soft crumb, great structure. Made in a dedicated GF space on Tuesday and Friday mornings.
