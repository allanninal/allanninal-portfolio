What we charge and how

Book time, and which book.

Labour is billed from published book time, not from a stopwatch. If the guide says an operation is 2.4 hours, that is what the invoice says whether it took us 1.8 or 3.1. That is normal across the trade and almost never explained, so here it is in writing along with which guide we use.

Free code reads

A code is a symptom, not a part.

A scan tool reads a code in about ninety seconds and plenty of places will do it for nothing. That is worth exactly what it costs, because a code names a symptom or a circuit — never a part.

Common OBD-II codes, what they state, what gets replaced, and what else causes them
CodeWhat it states What gets replacedWhat it also is
P0420 Catalyst efficiency below threshold, bank 1 Catalytic converter, $1,100–$1,900 A lazy upstream oxygen sensor, an exhaust leak before the sensor, or a misfire cooking the converter
P0171 System too lean, bank 1 Mass airflow sensor, $290–$480 An intake leak, a weak fuel pump, or a dirty MAF that only needed cleaning
P0300 Random or multiple cylinder misfire Coils and plugs, $400–$700 A vacuum leak, low fuel pressure, or one injector — the code will not tell you which
P0442 Small evaporative emission leak Purge valve, $180–$320 The fuel cap, roughly a third of the time, at no cost at all

Reading the code is free here too. Finding which of those it actually is takes a technician and a couple of hours, and that is what the diagnostic fee buys.

Common questions

Before you book.

Why publish a car where you were wrong?

Because a shop that only shows the times it was right is showing you an advert. Four of the seven things this customer declined were fine to decline. If we hid that, the two that were not fine would mean nothing.

Is book time fair?

It is the trade standard and it cuts both ways — you pay the same when a bolt shears and the job takes us an extra hour. What is not fair is not being told which guide is being used, so ours is on this page.

Why charge for a diagnosis when the code is free?

The code is not the diagnosis. P0420 is caused by the converter often enough that people replace it, and by three cheaper things often enough that replacing it first is how you spend the money twice. The fee buys the hours that tell those apart, and it comes off the repair.

Do you work on cars you did not sell parts for?

Yes. Bring your own parts if you want to — we will fit them, and we will say plainly that the warranty then covers our labour and not the part.

Will you tell me if something can wait?

Every recommendation we make comes with what happens if it does not get done, in words. Sixteen entries on this page and four of them are things we said could wait.

Do you do state inspections?

Iowa does not require periodic safety inspections for passenger vehicles, so there is nothing to sell you there. If you want a pre-purchase inspection on a car you are buying, that is $145 and takes about ninety minutes.
