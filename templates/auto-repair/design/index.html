One saturated colour, spent on one thing

Red only ever marks a consequence — a declined recommendation that came back and cost more. It appears twice on the whole demo page. If it were used for emphasis generally it would stop meaning that, and the ledger would lose the one signal it needs.

Every ledger entry states its status in text. The border style and the fill repeat it.
