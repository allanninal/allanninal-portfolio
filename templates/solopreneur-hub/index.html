About

The story behind the products

After years in product engineering, I started building solo. The freedom to pick problems, ship fast, and own 100% of the outcome is hard to give up. Today I run multiple products that generate recurring revenue — and I document everything publicly.

I write about the reality of solopreneur life — slow months, product pivots, and the compounding effect of showing up every day. If you're building solo, you're not alone.
