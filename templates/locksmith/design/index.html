#6E5410 — Decoration; the asterisk is announced in words

Two brasses, and why

#8A6A16 is 3.35:1 as text on the ground, which fails. So the token is fill-only — it is never used except with white on top — and a darker --color-accent-text carries brass wherever brass has to be read. A third value lifts it for the dark band. Splitting one brand colour into three tokens is cheaper than discovering the failure in an audit.

Matrix cells state a word

The tint behind each cell repeats what the word already says. Remove all colour and the grid still reads, which is the test.
