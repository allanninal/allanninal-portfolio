Team

The people who do the work.

Two principals and two designers. Everyone here is hands-on. We don't have account managers, BD reps, or strategists who don't make.

  • Allan Ninal

    Principal, Identity & Strategy

    Allan started Fieldwork in 2019 after eight years at a larger studio in Brooklyn. He leads identity engagements and writes most of the strategic positioning documents. Likes wood type, hates filler. Originally from Toronto.

    Designer
