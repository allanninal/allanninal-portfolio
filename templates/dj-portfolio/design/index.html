Reference

Design system

Every ratio was computed with tools/a11y/contrast.py against all three grounds. This page is noindex: it documents the template for whoever builds with it.

The first <audio> in the library

A hundred and eleven templates in, and this is the first with sound. Three click tracks generated with ffmpeg from a sine impulse and silence — not recordings, and not mixes. That distinction is the reason they can be here at all: the sets on this page are fictional, and a fabricated mix would be inventing a product, where a metronome at 126 BPM is just a metronome at 126 BPM.

Each player is a bare <audio controls>, which is keyboard-operable and labelled for free, with preload="none" so nobody who never presses play pays for three files. Every band states its BPM and its purpose in text, so the audio illustrates the point and never carries it — the page works identically with sound off, which is the same test the colour tokens have to pass.

Four of eleven, and that is the argument

The rider marks only four lines Required and the other seven Asked for, and the headline counts them from the data. A rider where everything is a must teaches a promoter nothing about what matters, so when something has to give they guess — and they guess wrong, because nobody told them the booth monitor was load-bearing and the water was not.

Required is red, and it is not the accent

Both rider states are a word and a glyph via data-glyph before they are a colour. On a table somebody is meant to tick, “required” must not share a hue with a heading — and --state-gone does double duty on the cost total, which is the other number on the page that should stop a reader.
