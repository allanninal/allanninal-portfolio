Learn & Progress

Intro clinics & private coaching

Every roped climber starts with a belay certification, and every plateau is worth a coach's eye. These packages take you from your first knot to projecting harder grades — led by our USAC-certified coaching team of Ryan Torres, Mei Chen, and Jake Hartwell.

Learn to Climb

Belay certification · all gear included

One 2-hour clinic · ages 8+

$35

The required starting point for top-rope climbing and the friendliest intro to the sport. Covers movement fundamentals, PBUS top-rope belay, harness fitting, and fall technique. You leave with your belay card and can climb independently.

  • ✓ Bouldering movement fundamentals
  • ✓ Top-rope belay certification (PBUS)
  • ✓ Shoes, harness & chalk included
  • ✓ Belay card valid same day
  • ✓ Offered six days a week

Youth Program

The Summit Youth Team

From first-time young climbers to competitive athletes chasing state finals, our youth track builds skills, strength, and confidence under Youth Program Director Mei Chen (USAC Level 2 Youth Coach, Wilderness First Responder). New athletes complete a short assessment before joining the competitive team.

Youth Recreational

Ages 6–17 · all abilities

1 session / week $95 / month

Structured, coach-led climbing without the competition pressure. Small groups learn movement, belay skills, and gym safety while building a love of the sport. The natural feeder into the competitive team.

  • ✓ One 90-min coached session weekly
  • ✓ Movement, belay & safety skills
  • ✓ Small-group, all-abilities coaching
  • ✓ Includes gym access on session days
  • ✓ Pathway into the competitive team

Development Squad

Ages 8–17 · by assessment

6 hrs / week $185 / month

The bridge to competition. Periodized training in strength, technique, and mental skills, with an introduction to comp formats. For committed climbers typically at V2+ and comfortable on top-rope.

  • ✓ Two coached sessions weekly (6 hrs)
  • ✓ Periodized strength & technique
  • ✓ Intro to competition formats
  • ✓ Included open-climb membership
  • ✓ Quarterly progress check-ins

Competitive Team

Ages 10–17 · coach invitation

10 hrs / week (in season) $295 / month

Our elite track for athletes competing in the Colorado Youth Climbing League and USAC regionals. Full periodized programming, competition strategy, video analysis, and travel support with Coach Mei.

  • ✓ Ten training hours weekly in season
  • ✓ Competition strategy & mental training
  • ✓ Video analysis & individual plans
  • ✓ USAC comp registration support
  • ✓ Quarterly outdoor climbing trips

The competitive season runs September–March (10 hrs/week), tapering to 6 hrs/week off-season. New athletes complete a brief movement assessment with Coach Mei before joining the Development Squad or Competitive Team. Sibling discounts and financial-aid spots are available each season — ask at the front desk. Youth memberships include open-climb access on training days.
