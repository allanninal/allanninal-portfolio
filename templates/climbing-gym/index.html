Bouldering Wall

Route grades for every level

Our bouldering wall uses the Hueco V-scale — the standard in North American gyms. Every route is color-coded by hold to match its grade band, so you always know what you're on. 118 active boulder problems across three caves.

V0–V1 Intro
V2–V3 Developing
V4–V5 Intermediate
V6–V7 Advanced
V8–V9 Expert
V10+ Elite
  • V0–V1 Intro 28 routes

    Large, positive holds with easy body positions. Perfect for your first session — no experience needed. Most people climb V0–V1 on day one.

  • V2–V3 Developing 32 routes

    Smaller holds and more complex movement sequences. Typically reached after a few months of regular climbing. Great benchmark for progressing beginners.

  • V4–V5 Intermediate 24 routes

    Demanding footwork, body tension, and dynamic movement. Climbers in this range have usually been training for 6–18 months. A major milestone.

  • V6–V7 Advanced 18 routes

    Precise technique, lock-off strength, and problem-specific tactics. Typically requires 1–3 years of dedicated training and structured coaching.

  • V8–V9 Expert 10 routes

    Campus-level power, full-body tension, and movement mastery. Fewer than 10% of gym climbers reach V8+. Competition-grade problems.

  • V10+ Elite 6 routes

    World-class difficulty. Our V10–V12 problems are set by national-level route setters. Reserved for elite athletes pursuing performance goals.

Top-Rope & Lead Walls

Our roped walls use the Yosemite Decimal System (YDS) and span 5.5 through 5.14b across 40 top-rope and 28 lead routes. Auto-belay lanes cover 5.6–5.11. Routes reset on a rolling 6–8 week cycle.

Do I need any experience to come for the first time?
None at all. Our bouldering walls are accessible from day one — just show up, get a waiver signed, and start climbing. No rope, no harness, no experience required for bouldering. If you want to use the top-rope or lead walls, you'll need to take our Learn to Climb clinic (2 hours, $35) to get your belay certification. You can book same-day if spots are available.
What's the difference between bouldering and top-rope?
Bouldering is roped-free climbing on shorter walls (typically 12–16 feet) with thick crash pads below. It's the most accessible entry point. Top-rope climbing uses ropes anchored at the top of taller walls (30–40 feet) and requires a belay partner with a belay certification. Lead climbing involves clipping the rope into draws as you ascend — it's the format used in outdoor sport climbing and requires additional certification beyond basic belay.
What gear do I need to bring?
Nothing — we have everything you need at the desk. Climbing shoes are $5 per session rental. Harnesses are $3. Chalk bags and chalk are free to use at the gym. If you plan to climb regularly, buying your own shoes is strongly recommended — our staff can help you find the right fit. We stock a full range of beginner to performance shoes in our gear shop.
How often are routes reset?
We reset sections of the bouldering and rope walls on a rolling 6–8 week cycle. The entire gym is typically fully reset every 3 months. Our head route setter Ryan Torres also sets new project-style problems on our competition wall on a monthly basis. We post reset schedules and new-route previews on Instagram a week in advance.
Is the Monthly Membership worth it compared to the Punch Card?
If you climb 4+ times per month, the Monthly Unlimited ($75) beats the Punch Card ($18/visit effectively). The membership also includes 15% off coached programs and 2 guest passes per month. If you travel frequently or climb 2–3 times per month, the Punch Card is more flexible because it never expires. We're happy to advise — just ask at the front desk or email us.
What age is appropriate for the Youth Climbing Team?
The team accepts athletes ages 8–17 who have demonstrated basic climbing competency (typically V2+ and comfortable on top-rope). New athletes do a brief assessment with Mei before joining. The team trains 10 hours per week during competition season (September–March) and 6 hours per week in the off-season. We also offer a Youth Recreational Program for ages 6+ who want structured climbing without competition focus.
Can I come in as a single visitor without a partner?
Absolutely. Our bouldering walls never require a partner. For top-rope, we have 4 auto-belay lanes that allow solo roped climbing up to 35 feet — no partner needed. Auto-belay requires a brief 5-minute orientation on your first visit. For lead climbing, you'll need a belay partner with a valid lead card.
What is your cancellation policy for classes?
We request 24 hours' notice for class cancellations. Cancellations with sufficient notice receive a full credit toward any future class booking. Cancellations under 24 hours or no-shows are charged in full. For the Lead Certification course (4-week), individual missed sessions may be made up in a subsequent cohort with coaching staff approval. Monthly membership holders who cancel also cancel all upcoming class bookings — credits are issued on a per-session basis.
