Start Here · No Commitment

The free intro consult

Every coaching relationship starts here. The intro consult is a no-pressure 45-minute conversation — in person at Embody Austin or over video — where I listen more than I talk. You leave knowing exactly what a program would look like for your specific situation, whether we end up working together or not.

  1. Step 1

    Your Goals & History

    We talk through what you actually want your life and body to look like six months out — and the training and nutrition history that got you here. The honest version, not the version that sounds good.

  2. Step 2

    My Honest Assessment

    I share what I think your first 12 weeks would realistically look like, which package fits your schedule and budget, and whether 1:1, small-group, or online is the right format for you.

  3. Step 3

    Your Next Step

    If it is a fit, we book your Kickoff Assessment and pick a package. If it is not, you still leave with a clear, specific idea of what to do next. Either way, the 45 minutes are yours.
