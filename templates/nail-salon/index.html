Clean & Safe

Our Commitment
to Your Safety

We hold ourselves to the highest hygiene standards — because beautiful nails should never come at a cost to your health.

◈

Single-Use Tools

Disposable files, buffers, and liners are used for each client — never reused.

◇

Medical-Grade Sterilisation

All metal implements are sterilised in a hospital-grade autoclave between every appointment.

◯

Non-Toxic Products

We use 5-free and 7-free polishes, low-odour acrylics, and dermatologist-tested gels.

✦

Clean Air Studio

HEPA filtration and individual nail desk ventilation keep the air fresh for clients and technicians alike.

Our Story

A Studio Built on
Artistry & Care

Petal & Polish was founded by Mia Chen in SoHo with a simple conviction: nail care should be an elevated experience — not rushed, not impersonal, and never at the expense of your health.

Every product we use is 5-free or better. Every tool is sterilised between clients. Every appointment begins with a consultation, because understanding your nails is the first step to transforming them.

We specialise in nail art that pushes creative boundaries — from hand-painted florals and chrome finishes to 3D sculptural designs — while always grounding our work in exceptional nail health and technique.
