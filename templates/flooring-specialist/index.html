What we install

Four materials, and what each really costs installed.

Ranges are dollars per square foot with materials, labour and basic prep together — the number you actually pay, not a labour-only teaser.

Solid & engineered hardwood

$6–$25 / sq ft installed

$10–$16 is where most Providence jobs land

Refinishable, and the only floor that adds to an appraisal in this housing stock. Species, grade and plank width move the number more than labour does.

Best for: Living rooms, dining rooms, bedrooms, main stairs

Luxury vinyl plank

$4.5–$12 / sq ft installed

The honest answer for most basements and baths

Waterproof wear layer, floats over most flat substrates, and the good ones no longer look like a rental. Cannot be refinished — when it wears, it is replaced.

Best for: Basements, kitchens, bathrooms, rentals, homes with big dogs

Porcelain & ceramic tile

$10–$31 / sq ft installed

Averages about $18 installed

The most durable thing we install and the most expensive to remove later. Layout and substrate drive the price far more than the tile you pick.

Best for: Bathrooms, mudrooms, entryways, radiant-heat rooms

Carpet & runners

$3–$11 / sq ft installed

Cheapest per foot, shortest life

Quiet, warm and forgiving underfoot. It is not a moral failure in a bedroom, whatever the renovation shows have told you.

Best for: Bedrooms, finished attics, stair runners, home offices

The part nobody photographs

The subfloor decides whether the floor lasts.

Every failed floor we are called to fix failed underneath. None of this work shows up in a before-and-after photo, which is exactly why it gets skipped.

01

Moisture, measured

Concrete gets an in-situ relative-humidity probe; wood subfloors get a pin meter. We record the numbers and give them to you. Manufacturers void warranties over exactly this, and "it looked dry" is not a reading.

02

Acclimation

Wood sits in your house, in the room it will live in, until its moisture content matches the space. Rushing this is the most common cause of gaps in January.

03

Flatness

Most manufacturers want 3/16" over 10 feet. Older Providence floors rarely start there, so the quote includes what levelling we can see — and says plainly what we cannot see until the old floor is up.

04

The old floor comes up

Carpet, tack strip, staples, and any layers a previous owner floated over. Homes here often have three generations of flooring stacked up. Disposal is in the number.

Pricing

What the number includes, and what changes it.

For scale: a 500 sq ft space runs roughly $1,529–$4,860 installed nationally, averaging about $3,160 — near $12.50 a square foot blended across materials.

In the square-foot price

  • ✓ Removal and disposal of the existing floor
  • ✓ Moisture testing, with the readings written down
  • ✓ Levelling up to 1/4" over 10 feet
  • ✓ Trim, transitions, thresholds and shoe moulding
  • ✓ Moving ordinary furniture — we do the lifting
  • ✓ Daily clean-up, and the dumpster

Priced separately, always in writing first

  • + Structural subfloor repair found after demo — quoted that day, in writing, before we continue
  • + Asbestos-backed sheet vinyl (common here pre-1985) — abatement is a licensed trade, not us
  • + Levelling beyond 1/4" over 10 feet
  • + Stair work, which is priced per tread rather than per foot
  • + Moving a piano, a safe, or a full-wall aquarium

Common questions

Before you call.

Do you sell the flooring, or just install it?

Both. We buy at trade pricing and pass the invoice through at cost, with our labour priced separately — so you can see what the material actually cost. You are also welcome to supply your own; the labour rate does not change.

Can I put hardwood in my basement?

Not solid hardwood, no. Below grade, slab moisture moves through concrete year-round and will cup solid wood within a couple of heating seasons. Engineered wood is sometimes viable over a tested slab with a vapour barrier; LVP nearly always is. We will test before answering.

How long does a floor take?

A single room is usually a day or two. A main level runs three to five days, plus acclimation time before we start — typically three to five days for solid wood. Refinishing adds drying time between coats and you will want to be out of the house for it.

What is the mess like?

Demo is loud and dusty. Sanding is worse, though we run dust containment and it is far better than it was ten years ago. We seal off the rooms we are not working in, and we clean daily rather than at the end.

My house is from 1910 and nothing is level. Is that a problem?

It is normal here, and it is the reason the quote has a levelling line in it. We measure the deflection before quoting, so the allowance is based on your floor rather than an average. What we cannot promise is what is under the old floor until it is up.

Do you do stairs?

Yes, priced per tread rather than per square foot — a staircase is joinery, not flooring, and pretending otherwise is how people end up with a change order.

Is there a warranty?

Two years on our labour. Material warranties come from the manufacturer and most of them hinge on documented moisture readings and acclimation, which is exactly why we record ours and hand them to you.

Do you take on small jobs?

A single room, a closet, a landing — yes. There is a minimum that covers a day on site, and we will tell you what it is on the phone rather than after the visit.
