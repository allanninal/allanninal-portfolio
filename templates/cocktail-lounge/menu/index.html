Full Cocktail List

The Velvet Cipher

Signature Cocktails

  • Dead Drop

    $19

    Smoky Oaxacan mezcal meets a house-made roasted rice tea syrup and tart yuzu — served over a single obsidian sphere ice, finished with a charcoal-dusted rim. The cipher begins here.

  • Emerald Cypher

    $18

    Indigo-hued Empress gin balanced by herbal Chartreuse, muddled cucumber and basil — crowned with a delicate lime-acid foam. Complex, botanical, electric.

  • Noir Negroni

    $17

    A brooding riff on the classic — Scotch replaces gin, Campari is cold-brew-infused for depth, and black walnut bitters add earthiness. A 40-second stir over hand-carved ice.

  • Velvet Storm

    $18

    Rich aged rum shaken with ripe passion fruit and fragrant cardamom syrup, softened with house coconut cream and finished with an overproof rum float. Dense, tropical, dangerous.

  • Smoke Signal

    $20

    Peated Islay whisky softened by a house honey-thyme cordial and fresh lemon, dry-shaken for a silky egg-white foam, then kissed with a cedar smoke mist tableside.

  • Cipher Sour

    $16

    Pisco shaken with fresh lychee, a breath of rose water, and aquafaba for a silky vegan foam. Three dashes of Peychaud's on the surface. Floral, bright, seductive.

  • Ghost Old Fashioned

    $21

    Fat-washed bourbon meets Haitian Clairin for a ghost-like funkiness. Coconut oil wash, pure vanilla sugar, two dashes Angostura. Stirred to perfection over a hand-chiseled block.

  • Last Transmission

    $17

    High-proof navy-strength gin with a house kaffir lime leaf and turmeric shrub — golden, aromatic, served long over ice with a light tonic mist. Citrus-forward with a lingering heat.

Classics, Cracked

  • Dirty Martini

    $16

    Stirred until it's cold enough to hurt. Three-to-one ratio, expressed lemon peel, house-cured olive on a steel pick.

  • Negroni

    $15

    Equal parts. Stirred. Orange peel. Non-negotiable.

  • Daiquiri

    $14

    The platonic daiquiri — rum, lime, sugar, nothing else. Shaken hard, served up in a chilled coupe. Deceptively simple, ruthlessly executed.

  • Paper Plane

    $15

    Sam Ross's modern classic — four equal parts, shaken, strained. Bittersweet, tart, unforgettable.

  • Whisky Sour

    $14

    The original sour — rye whisky balanced against fresh citrus and sweetness, shaken with egg white for froth. Angostura on top.

  • Espresso Martini

    $16

    Double-shot cold brew, vodka, Kahlúa — dry-shaken for a thick crema. The after-midnight recalibration.

Zero-Proof

  • Phantom Spritz

    $12

    Bright bergamot and white grapefruit over ice with a floral elderflower tonic. Gorgeous, complex, and entirely alcohol-free. Garnished with a dehydrated citrus wheel.

  • Dark Signal

    $12

    Smoky and complex without a drop of spirit — cold brew with a tamarind-date reduction, coconut milk, and non-alcoholic bitters. Looks like the night sky. Tastes like dessert.

  • Jade Garden

    $11

    Freshly pressed cucumber with cold-brewed green tea, yuzu juice, and a light honey syrup. Long over ice, topped with soda. Refreshing and herbal.

Small Bites

  • Oysters on Ice (Half Dozen)

    $26

    Market oysters, mignonette, Tabasco honey, lime. The ideal Dead Drop companion.

  • Wagyu Sliders (×2)

    $22

    Wagyu beef on brioche, truffle aioli, caramelized onion, aged cheddar, house pickles.

  • Cipher Board

    $28

    Rotating selection of three cured meats, two cheeses, honeycomb, grain mustard, cornichons, artisan crackers.

  • Truffle & Parmesan Fries

    $14

    Hand-cut double-fried potato, black truffle oil, shaved parmesan, smoked flaky salt.

  • Tuna Tataki

    $24

    Seared yellowfin, ponzu, avocado cream, sesame, microgreens, crispy wonton strips.

  • Wild Mushroom Crostini

    $16

    Chanterelle and black trumpet mushrooms, whipped ricotta, thyme oil, aged balsamic on sourdough crostini.

The list rotates with the seasons — ask your bartender about tonight's off-menu builds. Must be 21+ to be served.
