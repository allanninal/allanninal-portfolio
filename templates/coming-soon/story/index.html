The story

Why we’re making Maison Nouvelle

For years I walked past the same empty corner shop and imagined what it could be. Not another place that rushes you in and out, but somewhere worth slowing down for — considered, quiet, made by hand.

Maison Nouvelle started as a notebook full of swatches, half-finished sketches, and a stubborn belief that the small things still matter. The weight of a door handle. The way light falls across a counter at four in the afternoon. The feeling of being somewhere that was made on purpose.

We’re not in a hurry. Everything you’ll see when we open has been chosen, reworked, and chosen again. If that sounds like your kind of place, you found us at exactly the right moment — before the doors, while the paint is still drying.

When we open, the people on this list come first. That’s the whole point of being early.

Allan Ninal

Founder, Maison Nouvelle
