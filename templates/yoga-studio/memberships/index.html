Yoga Alliance RYT-200

200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training

Deepen your practice — or begin your path to teaching.

Our flagship 200-hour training is a Yoga Alliance–registered program led by Sofia Andersen (E-RYT-500) and the full Sattva faculty. Whether you intend to teach or simply want to understand your practice from the inside out, you will leave with a certification recognized worldwide and a practice transformed.

The curriculum — five pillars, 200 hours

  • Asana, Alignment & Sequencing

    75 hrs

    The mechanics and art of the postures — safe alignment, hands-on and verbal adjustments, modifications for every body, and how to build a class that flows with intention from opening to savasana.

  • Anatomy & Physiology

    30 hrs

    Functional anatomy for movement — the spine, joints, and fascia; the breath and nervous system; common contraindications; and how to keep students safe without teaching from fear.

  • Philosophy, Ethics & History

    30 hrs

    The roots of yoga beyond the mat — the Yoga Sutras, the eight limbs, the yamas and niyamas, and the ethical responsibility of holding space as a teacher in a modern studio.

  • Pranayama, Meditation & Subtle Body

    30 hrs

    Breathwork techniques, seated and guided meditation, and an introduction to the koshas, chakras, and the energetic frameworks that give the physical practice its depth.

  • Teaching Methodology & Practicum

    35 hrs

    The craft of teaching — voice, cueing, demonstration, and presence. You will teach real classes to your peers and mentors, receive feedback, and graduate ready to lead a room.

Your certification & what comes after

Graduates who complete all contact hours, the practicum, and the take-home requirements receive a Sattva 200-Hour certificate and are eligible to register with Yoga Alliance as an RYT-200 — the credential recognized by studios worldwide.

  • ✓ Yoga Alliance RYT-200 eligibility on completion
  • ✓ A written manual and lifetime access to course recordings
  • ✓ Guaranteed audition slot to join the Sattva teaching roster
  • ✓ Ongoing mentorship and a graduate community for continued growth

Workshops, immersions & retreats

Step off the weekly schedule and go deeper into a single theme. Half-day workshops, seasonal immersions, and our twice-yearly retreat in the Columbia Gorge.

  • Workshop $65

    The Art of Yin: Fascia & Stillness

    Third Saturday, monthly · 3 hours

    A deep dive into the Yin practice — the anatomy of fascia, the philosophy of long holds, and how to meet intensity with breath rather than force. Suitable for all levels.

    With James Okoro

  • Immersion $85

    Prenatal & Postnatal Immersion

    One Sunday per season · 4 hours

    A supportive half-day for expectant and new mothers — safe sequencing by trimester, breath for labor, pelvic-floor awareness, and gentle restoration for the postpartum body.

    With Sofia Andersen

  • Workshop $55

    Inversions & Arm Balances

    Quarterly · 2.5 hours

    Demystify crow, headstand, and forearm balance with drills, spotting, and a fear-free, progression-based approach. Bring curiosity — no prior inversion experience required.

    With Maya Chen

  • Retreat $395

    Restore & Renew Weekend Retreat

    Twice yearly — Hood River, OR · 2 days / 1 night

    A restorative weekend away from the city — slow morning flows, long restorative and Yin sessions, guided meditation, whole-food meals, and unhurried time in the Columbia Gorge.

    With Lena Müller & faculty
