Finding the studio — by appointment only

  1. From NY-9, turn onto County Rd 21 heading west.
  2. After about 2.3 miles, turn left (south) onto Kettlebrook Farm Road — gravel, easy to miss.
  3. The studio is the converted barn at 118, third driveway on the right. Look for the red mailbox marked “Calder Studio Pottery.”
  4. Park along the fence line; the kiln shed is the smaller building behind the barn.
