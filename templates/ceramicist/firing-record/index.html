Firing №44

2026-06-08 · Stoneware · cone 6, standard ramp · 10.0% lost.

Ramp schedule

Candling / water smoking
Room temp → 500°F, door cracked, low heat, to drive off residual moisture before it can flash to steam inside the wall.
Climb to cone 6
500°F → 2232°F (1222°C) at the kiln's standard setting, 108°F/hr.
Hold at peak
10 minutes at cone 6, oxidation.
Cool
Natural cool, kiln off, lid/door shut until below 200°F — never forced through the 573°C (1063°F) quartz inversion, the point most dunting cracks below happened when that rule was cut short.

Glazes loaded

  • Satin Matte White (Cone 6, satin matte) — food-safe, dishwasher- and microwave-safe, absorption < 0.5%.
  • Cobalt Speckle (Cone 6, glossy) — food-safe, dishwasher- and microwave-safe, absorption < 0.5%.
  • Amber Celadon (Cone 5–6, glossy) — food-safe, dishwasher- and microwave-safe, absorption < 0.5%.
  • Honey Speckle (Cone 6, matte) — food-safe, dishwasher- and microwave-safe, absorption < 0.5%.

What failed

  • S-crack (1) — A trimmed mug base, dried too fast under the studio fan — the crack opened at bisque, not glaze.
