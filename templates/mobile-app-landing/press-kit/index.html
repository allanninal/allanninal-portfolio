About Flowra

Flowra is a warm, gentle habit tracker that turns “I should” into “I did” — one streak at a time. Built for people who've bounced off guilt-driven productivity apps, it pairs a calm interface with grace days, home-screen widgets, and a six-month consistency view that respects your time.

Screens

    Your habits, in order, as they fit your day.
