Features

Everything you need to keep showing up.

No coaching modules. No streak shaming. Just the quiet machinery of consistency.

  • Streak tracking

    Each day you check in, your streak grows. Miss one — Flowra holds your place once a month so a sick day doesn't cost you a year.

  • Smart reminders

    Reminders that learn your routine. Wake-up coffee at 7:14? Flowra knows. Yoga every Tuesday? Got it.

  • Home-screen widgets

    iOS and Android widgets show today's habits at a glance. Tap to check off without unlocking the app.

  • Progress stats

    Six-month consistency view, longest streaks per habit, weekday heatmaps. Numbers without judgement.

  • iCloud + Drive sync

    Your habits sync across iPhone, iPad, Android tablet, and Apple Watch. End-to-end encrypted.

  • Dark mode

    Yes, the app has dark mode. (This landing page is light-only by design — read about it in the FAQ.)

How it works

Three steps. No onboarding survey.

  1. 01

    Set a habit

    Pick something small — drink water, read 10 pages, walk after lunch. Anything you can do in under 5 minutes.

  2. 02

    Check it off

    Tap the circle when you've done it. That's the entire interaction. No notes, no scoring, no dopamine dance.

  3. 03

    Watch the streak grow

    Day by day the number ticks up. After two weeks you stop needing reminders. That's the goal.

FAQ

Things people ask before they download.

  • Is Flowra free? +

    Yes — the app is free to download and use. There's an optional Flowra+ subscription ($1.99/mo) that unlocks unlimited habits, widget themes, and Apple Watch complications. The free tier covers everything you need to build five daily habits.

  • Is Flowra on iOS, Android, or both? +

    Both. The iPhone version came first; Android arrived in 2024. The two clients sync to the same backend, so you can switch devices without losing your streak.

  • Will my habits sync across devices? +

    Yes — via iCloud (iOS / iPadOS / macOS) and Google Drive (Android). Your habit data is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your device. Flowra's server only holds opaque blobs.

  • Can I add custom habits or only pick from a list? +

    Anything you want. Habits are short text strings — "20 push-ups," "call mom," "no Coke today." Add them on the fly; rename them anytime.

  • Is my data private? Do you sell it? +

    No. Your habits are encrypted before sync. Flowra does not have access to them. We don't sell, share, or aggregate user data — and there is no analytics SDK in the app. Privacy details live in the in-app settings and our Privacy page.
