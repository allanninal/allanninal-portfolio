How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 01

    A short call

    Thirty minutes, no charge. You describe the problem; I tell you honestly whether I am the right person and which engagement fits.

  2. 02

    A written scope

    Before any invoice, you get a one-page scope: the outcome, what is in, what is explicitly out, the timeline, and the price. No surprises.

  3. 03

    The work, in the open

    I work in your repo against your CI, with a short written update every week. You can see exactly where things stand at any point.

  4. 04

    Handover that sticks

    Every engagement ends with docs, tests, and a walkthrough so the work keeps paying off long after the contract closes.

Questions

Before you reach out.

Do you take equity instead of cash?
Occasionally, for advisory retainers with early-stage teams I believe in. Never as the whole fee, and never for fixed-scope project work.
Can you just join our standups full-time?
No. I do outcome-scoped work, not staff augmentation. If you need a full-time engineer, you need a hire, and I am happy to help you interview for one.
What stacks do you work in?
TypeScript and Go day to day; comfortable across Postgres, Kubernetes, Bazel-style build systems, and most major CI platforms. The work is rarely about the language.
How do payments work?
Fixed-fee engagements are 50% to start, 50% on delivery. Retainers bill monthly in advance. Standard net-15 terms, invoices via Stripe.
