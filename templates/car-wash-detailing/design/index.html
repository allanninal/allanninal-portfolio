The gap was lightness, not hue

All 89 grounds shipped before this were measured. They sit either above 90% lightness (35 tinted creams and pales) or below 10% (17 dark slates) — nothing in between. This one is at 71%. A first draft used dark slate with an amber accent and was discarded for landing within a few degrees of hvac-service, which is already dark slate with a tan accent.

What a mid ground costs

Muted text nearly stops working, so --color-text-muted was solved against the darker of the two grounds rather than the lighter one, and borders were pushed to #4C555B to clear 3:1 as structural UI. Amber is a fill only — as text on the ground it is 1.8:1 and would fail, so it never appears that way. Hierarchy comes from weight and rule thickness instead of lightness, which suits a price board.

The break-even is arithmetic

The amber cell on the board is ceil(monthly / single), computed in the page. Change a price in config.ts and the claim follows it. It is the one number on a wash tariff that is normally not printed.
