Hard-Water Guide

The most-argued-about job in this trade. A customer pays to have mineral staining removed, it does not come off, and both sides think they are right — because the answer depends on whether the mineral sits on the glass or has etched into it. That is testable in about a minute.

The four stages

Hard-water staining stages, appearance, outcome and cost
StageHow it looksOutcomeCost
Stage 1 — surface deposit Wipes to a smear with vinegar on a cloth; the smear clears. Polishes out fully. $8 – $20 per pane
Stage 2 — bonded scale Vinegar dulls it but a chalky bloom stays. Fingernail catches slightly. Usually polishes out. Takes three to four times as long. $25 – $50 per pane
Stage 3 — early etch Nail catches clearly. Wet glass looks clean; dry glass shows the pattern again. Partial improvement only. We will show you one pane before quoting the rest. $40 – $75 per pane
Stage 4 — full etch Visible pitting under raking light. Wetting the glass changes nothing. Nothing removes this. The glass is the damage. Replacement, not cleaning

The one-minute test

  1. Wet a small square with white vinegar and leave it 60 seconds
  2. Wipe with a clean microfibre and let it dry fully
  3. Look across the glass at a low angle, with light behind it
  4. Run a fingernail over the boundary between treated and untreated
  5. If the treated square is clear, it is stage 1–2. If the pattern returns as it dries, it is stage 3+

What causes it

Irrigation overspray
The single biggest cause we see. A head aimed a few inches high will etch a ground-floor pane in three summers.
Salt spray left through a season
Coastal glass that goes a full year between cleans starts bonding.
Concrete and stucco runoff
Alkaline, and far more aggressive than tap water. New builds are the usual victims.
Pressure washing the siding
Drives mineral-laden water onto glass and then dries it in the sun. Cover the windows first.

Preventing it

  • ✓ Re-aim sprinkler heads away from glass — the cheapest fix in this entire guide
  • ✓ Do not let coastal glass go more than five months in summer
  • ✓ Rinse windows before washing the siding, and rinse again after
  • ✓ Squeegee shower glass; the same chemistry, in miniature, every day
  • ✓ Ask about a sacrificial coating on west-facing panes if irrigation cannot be moved
