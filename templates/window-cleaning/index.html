Local honesty

Why Portland glass films faster.

An inland house at the same price point is fine on two visits a year. On the peninsula it is three, and the reason is different every season.

Spring Pine pollen
Two heavy weeks in late May. It bakes on, and rain makes it worse before it makes it better.
Summer Irrigation spray
The single most common cause of permanent mineral etching we see. Check where your heads point.
Autumn Salt and storm spray
Onshore wind carries salt well past the waterfront. Deering gets it too, just slower.
Winter Road sand and wood smoke
Sand pits the lower panes; soot films the upper ones. Different problems, different passes.

Common questions

Before you book.

How often does Portland glass actually need doing?

Three times a year on the peninsula, twice off it. Salt is the reason — an inland house at the same price point would be fine on two. We would rather tell you that than sell you four.

Do you clean in winter?

Yes, down to about 25°F with the right solution. Not in ice, not in high wind, and not when the forecast says the glass will freeze before it dries.

What happens if it rains the next day?

Clean glass sheds rain and stays clear. What makes windows look bad after rain is the dirt that was already there. If yours spot badly within 48 hours, call us and we will come back.

Can you get hard-water stains out?

Sometimes. If the minerals sit on the glass, a cerium polish removes them. If they have etched into it, nothing removes them and anyone who says otherwise is about to take your money. We test one pane and show you before quoting the rest.

Do I need to be home?

For exterior only, no. For interiors, yes, or leave access arranged. We text on the way and send a photo of the finished glass either way.

Why is your quote higher than the one I got yesterday?

Almost always scope. Ask the other quote whether screens, tracks and sills are included. If they are, and it is still cheaper, take it — genuinely.
