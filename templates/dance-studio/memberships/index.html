The New Student Starter Pass

$492 weeks unlimited

New to Éclat? Start here. The New Student Starter Pass is two weeks of unlimited classes for $49 — try every discipline, meet the faculty, and find where you belong before you commit to a membership. Adults get their very first class free before the pass even begins.

  1. Step 1

    Your First Class, Free

    Every adult begins with a free first class in any discipline — no card, no commitment. Come see the studio, the teaching style, and the level before anything else. Children start with a $15 trial, credited to the pass.

  2. Step 2

    Placement & Plan

    Our front desk helps you (or your child) find the right level and a class mix for the fortnight — whether that's three ballet classes or a taste of all five disciplines. No experience is ever assumed.

  3. Step 3

    Two Weeks Unlimited

    Dance as much as you like for 14 days across every discipline on the schedule. At the end, roll your $49 straight into a membership within 30 days and we credit it toward your first month.

Claim Your Starter Pass

First-time Éclat students only, one per person. The pass activates on your first booked class and runs 14 consecutive days. Children under 18 begin with a $15 trial class (credited toward the pass). Apply your Starter Pass to any membership within 30 days and we credit the $49 toward your first month.

Éclat Performance Programs

Take the stage — company, competition & pre-professional tracks

Beyond the weekly schedule, Éclat runs audition-based programs for dancers who want to compete, perform, and train toward a professional path. Each track adds to your membership and is coached by the faculty member who lives in that world — Sofia for classical and conservatory prep, Elena for DanceSport, Marcus for commercial repertoire. Every track works toward the annual Spring Showcase and select external competitions.

The Éclat Performing Arts Scholarship

01

Merit Awards Up to 50% off

Awarded each spring by faculty audition to youth-company and pre-professional dancers who show exceptional promise, regardless of ability to pay full tuition.

02

Access Grants Need-based

Confidential, need-based tuition support so cost is never the reason a committed young dancer stops training. Applications are reviewed twice yearly by the studio directors.

03

Showcase Costume Fund Recital support

Covers the one-time Spring Showcase costume and recital fee for scholarship families, so every enrolled student can take the Athenaeum stage in May.

04

Community Class Places Free places

A set number of free class places each term, offered in partnership with Lincoln Park schools to introduce new young dancers to the studio.
