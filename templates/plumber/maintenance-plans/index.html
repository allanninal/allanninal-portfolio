Priority Service SLA

How fast we get to you — in writing.

Membership is not just a discount card — it changes where you sit in the dispatch queue. Every Care Club member gets a written response-time commitment, and true plumbing emergencies (burst pipes, sewage backups, no water) always take priority regardless of plan. If we miss a same-day window on a Whole-Home plan for reasons within our control, that visit’s dispatch is on us.

  • 1-hour dispatch

    Emergency

    All plans · 24/7/365

    Burst pipes, sewage backups, flooding, or total water loss. A licensed plumber is dispatched within the hour, any hour — members are routed ahead of non-member emergency calls.

  • Booked by noon = same day

    Same-Day

    Whole-Home members

    Non-emergency repairs booked before noon are completed the same day, guaranteed. Miss the window on us and the dispatch fee is credited back to your account automatically.

  • Next business day

    Priority Next-Day

    Home Protection members

    Standard repairs are scheduled the next business day, ahead of non-members. You pick the arrival window; we hold it with a 30-minute text-ahead call.

  • Within 3 business days

    Scheduled

    Basic Care members

    Routine, non-urgent work is booked within three business days at a time that suits you — still ahead of the general public queue, still with the waived dispatch fee.

Response windows are measured from the time we confirm your request during operating hours and assume safe, accessible working conditions and available parts. Weather events and utility outages affecting the whole metro may extend windows; we will always call ahead with an honest ETA rather than leave you waiting.

The Blue Ridge Guarantee

Backed in writing, standing behind every job.

Every repair and installation we perform is protected by our written workmanship guarantee, and Care Club members receive extended coverage. If our work fails, we come back — no invoice, no argument. That accountability is why Mike put his own Master Plumber license behind the company name.

01

Workmanship Warranty

1 year standard · 2 years for members

If a repair fails because of our workmanship, we return and make it right at no charge for labor. Care Club members get that coverage doubled to two full years on every invoice.

02

Parts & Fixtures

Manufacturer warranty honored

We install professional-grade parts and register the manufacturer warranty in your name — typically 1–5 years. New water heaters carry a separate 6-year tank warranty we service directly.

03

Upfront-Price Promise

Quoted before we start

The flat-rate price is approved in writing before any work begins. No hourly surprises, no “it took longer than expected” markups — the number you approve is the number you pay.

04

Satisfaction Guarantee

100% or we make it right

If you are not satisfied with a completed job, tell us within 30 days and we will re-do the work or refund the labor. Members also get their annual dispatch fees credited if we miss an SLA window.

Guarantees cover the specific work performed on the invoice and exclude damage from misuse, unrelated failures, or work altered by others after our visit. Licensed under NC Master Plumber License #MP-48293 and fully insured — verify our license anytime at nclbgc.org. Membership guarantees are active for the plan term and require the annual inspection to be completed on schedule.
