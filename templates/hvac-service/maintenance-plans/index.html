Member Priority Service

When you call, members go to the front of the line.

A maintenance plan is not just discounts — it is a written service commitment. Members are dispatched ahead of non-members, get guaranteed response windows, and never meet an after-hours surprise on the invoice. Here is exactly what we promise, in writing.

Emergency dispatch

Non-member Within 60 minutes
Plan member Front-of-line, within 45 minutes

No heat, no cooling, a carbon monoxide alarm or a furnace locking out — a technician answers, day or night.

Non-emergency service call

Non-member Next available slot
Plan member Same day when booked before noon

Members are slotted ahead of the general queue on the same working day.

After-hours premium

Non-member Applies evenings & weekends
Plan member Waived on Comfort Club and above

February at midnight costs a member the same as a Tuesday morning.

Seasonal visit scheduling

Non-member You call us
Plan member We call you, twice a year

Booked ahead of the changeover rather than during the rush, when every contractor in the metro is already committed.

Repair labour warranty

Non-member One year
Plan member Two years, three on Whole-Home

Covers our workmanship on any repair carried out while the plan is active.

No heat or no cooling right now? Members go to the front of the line — but we answer every call live, 24/7. Call (816) 555-0193.

Response windows describe our dispatch commitment during normal operating conditions. A regional weather event — the first hard freeze, or a run of days above 100°F — puts every contractor in the metro under load; members keep their place at the front of our queue, but the clock may stretch. We will always tell you an honest arrival time rather than an optimistic one.

Warranties & Guarantees

What we stand behind, and for how long.

Meridian Air backs its work with a written workmanship warranty on top of the manufacturer coverage on parts and equipment. Maintenance-plan members get an extended labour warranty and a satisfaction guarantee on every visit.

01

Workmanship warranty

1 year standard · up to 3 for members

If a repair fails because of how we did it, we come back and put it right at no charge. Members carry two years, or three on the Whole-Home Plan.

02

Installation warranty

10 years on labour

Complete system installs carry a decade of labour coverage alongside the manufacturer equipment warranty — provided the annual maintenance is documented, which is what the plan is for.

03

Right-size guarantee

On every replacement

If a system we sized and installed cannot hold the temperature you asked it to hold on a design day, we correct it — equipment, ductwork or both — at our cost. That is what the Manual J is for.

04

No-surprise pricing

Every job, every time

You approve a flat-rate price before any work starts. If the job turns out to need more than we quoted, that is our estimate to absorb, not your invoice to inherit.

Warranty coverage applies to work performed by Meridian Air and excludes damage from misuse, unpermitted alterations by others, rodent or water intrusion, and acts of nature. The extended member labour warranty applies to work completed while your plan is active and in good standing. Equipment warranties are provided by the manufacturer and generally require documented annual maintenance to remain valid. Full terms are provided in your written service agreement at enrolment.
