The Brief

A spatial identity for a studio that thinks in structure.

Visual identity for a Scandinavian architecture studio: wordmark, spatial application system, stationery, and a brand manual worth reading.

Forma brand identity stationery — white letterhead with minimal wordmark

Approach

Design decisions for Forma

01

Material Research

Before any mark-making, a thorough audit of the category — competitor brands, reference materials, production constraints, and the client's own touchpoints.

02

Direction Development

Three distinct visual directions presented in context — not in isolation. Each direction has its own logic, its own risks, and its own version of the client's future.

03

Production Oversight

Design that only exists in Figma isn't finished. Specification, printer liaison, press approval, and delivery of a complete asset library with usage guidelines.
