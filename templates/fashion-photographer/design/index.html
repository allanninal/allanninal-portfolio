<details> as a content device

The library has only ever used disclosure for FAQ accordions. Here it is the page's second structural layer: the credit block is the public record and stays open, the economics are the thing a reader chooses to look at. Native, keyboard-operable, and it works with JavaScript off — which a test asserts rather than assumes.

Everything countable is counted

Net is fee minus costs, computed. The crew list is derived from the credit blocks, so it cannot name anyone the frames do not. The "N of 5 lost money" line counts the negative nets. And the headline claim — eight to fifteen people per image — is asserted by a test against the blocks themselves, because a claim the page's own data contradicts is worse than no claim.
