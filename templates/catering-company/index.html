Our Story

A Decade of Culinary Excellence

Lumière Catering was founded in 2012 by Chef Isabelle Montier, a Culinary Institute of America graduate who believed that truly extraordinary catering meant treating every event like a Michelin-starred restaurant service.

What began as a boutique wedding catering service in Manhattan has grown into a full-service team of 18 in-house chefs, sommeliers, and event coordinators — collectively holding over 90 years of culinary experience.

We source our ingredients from local New York farms and trusted sustainable suppliers, building relationships that give us access to the finest seasonal produce, heritage-breed proteins, and artisanal products.
