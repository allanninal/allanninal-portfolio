Wires involved
Call the utility first, not us. We cannot legally touch a tree on a live conductor.
Tree on the structure
Leave the house if anything is creaking. Call us; we work these overnight.
Tree down, nothing hit
It can wait for daylight and a standard rate. That is worth several hundred dollars.
Leaning but standing
Rope off the drop zone at 1.5× the tree height and wait for an assessment.

Section 7 — Questions

Before you call.

Do I need an assessment, or can you just quote a removal?

We can quote a removal, but we will look at the tree properly first and tell you if it does not need to come down. Roughly a third of the trees we are called to assess get a recommendation to keep them. That is not generosity — it is what the assessment is for.

What does ISA Certified actually mean?

An exam, continuing education, and a code of ethics through the International Society of Arboriculture. TRAQ is a separate qualification specifically for risk assessment. Neither is a licence, and plenty of competent climbers hold neither — but if someone is telling you a tree is dangerous, it is fair to ask what training that judgement rests on.

Will insurance pay for this?

Usually only if the tree hit something insured. A tree down in the yard that hit nothing is typically your cost. Sublimits are commonly $500–$1,000 per tree and around $5,000 total, so a large removal often exceeds what the policy pays.

Why is storm work more expensive?

A 25–50% surcharge, because it is night work, in wind, often with the tree under load and next to something it has already damaged. It is the most dangerous work we do and it is priced accordingly.

Can you save a tree that is already leaning?

Sometimes. A tree that has always leaned is different from one that started last week — the second is a root failure and usually not salvageable. We look for soil cracking and a lifted root plate, which is why we ask when you first noticed.

Do you top trees?

No. Topping removes the crown's structure, forces weak regrowth, and creates the hazard you were trying to avoid. Any company willing to top a tree for you is telling you something about how they work.
