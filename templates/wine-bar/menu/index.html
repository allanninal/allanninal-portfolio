Maison Velours

Red

From cool-climate pinots to structured Jura blends — reds with presence but not aggression.

  • Les Singuliers Rouge Domaine Overnoy-Crinquand · Arbois, Jura · 2022

    $18 / $88

    Poulsard / Trousseau

    Translucent garnet. Wild strawberry, dried violet, iron filings. Gossamer tannins — almost weightless. A Jura classic for those who seek depth without density.

  • Pommard 1er Cru "Les Pézerolles" Domaine Courbet · Côte de Beaune, Burgundy · 2020

    $145

    Pinot Noir

    Ruby-red with brick at the rim. Black cherry compote, forest floor, spice box. Fine but present tannins with a long, mineral finish. Bottle-only for contemplation.

  • Terre Siciliane Nero d'Avola COS Cerasuolo di Vittoria · Sicily · 2021

    $16 / $74

    Nero d'Avola / Frappato

    Deep ruby. Cherry jam, sun-dried tomato, cocoa nib. Mediterranean herbs on the finish. Warm, generous, and endlessly food-friendly.

  • Saumur-Champigny "Marginale" Thierry Germain / Domaine des Roches Neuves · Loire Valley · 2022

    $17 / $82

    Cabernet Franc

    Bright crimson. Violet, fresh raspberry, graphite, green pepper. Silky and precise — the ideal Cabernet Franc for warm evenings and cool conversation.

White

Mineral, textural, alive. Whites chosen for complexity — not just refreshment.

  • Muscadet Sèvre et Maine Sur Lie "Clisson" Domaine de la Pepière · Loire Valley · 2021

    $15 / $68

    Melon de Bourgogne

    Pale gold. Saline, citrus zest, wet stone, brioche from extended lees aging. Lean yet layered — the oyster wine of choice.

  • Puligny-Montrachet "Les Charmes" Étienne Sauzet · Côte de Beaune, Burgundy · 2021

    $178

    Chardonnay

    Gold-tinged. Ripe pear, hazelnut, cream, white flowers, and a razor-sharp acidity that gives the wine incredible length. A Burgundy benchmark.

  • Grüner Veltliner Smaragd "Achleiten" Franz Hirtzberger · Wachau · 2022

    $19 / $92

    Grüner Veltliner

    Pale straw. White pepper, grapefruit, peach, mineral sheen. Full-bodied but never heavy. Smaragd ripeness with Austrian precision.

  • Txakoli de Getaria Ameztoi · Basque Country · 2023

    $14 / $58

    Hondarrabi Zuri

    Nearly transparent. Tart green apple, saline spray, lemon verbena. Low alcohol, high refreshment. Serve slightly sparkling, very cold.

Sparkling

Pét-nats, grower Champagnes, and everything the bubble world has been hiding from you.

  • Vouvray Pét-Nat "L'Ancestrale" Domaine Huet · Loire Valley · 2022

    $16 / $72

    Chenin Blanc

    Hazy golden. Off-dry and alive with quince, fresh brioche, and a lively mousse. Ancestral method — one fermentation, lightly cloudy, honest.

  • Blanc de Blancs Extra Brut Pierre Péters — Récoltant-Manipulant · Le Mesnil-sur-Oger, Champagne · NV

    $24 / $118

    Chardonnay

    Bright gold, fine persistent bead. Chalk, green apple, Meyer lemon, almond. The definitive grower Champagne — mineral authority without compromise.

  • Crémant d'Alsace Blanc de Blancs Domaine Valentin Zusslin · Alsace · NV

    $13 / $52

    Auxerrois / Pinot Blanc

    Pale straw. Apple blossom, pear, gentle brioche toast. Fine mousse, lingering. Great value entry into Alsatian terroir bubbles.

Orange

Extended skin contact whites from the world's most adventurous winemakers. Amber, tannic, unforgettable.

  • Rkatsiteli Qvevri Amber Pheasant's Tears · Kakheti · 2021

    $20 / $96

    Rkatsiteli

    Deep amber-gold. Dried apricot, beeswax, chamomile, tannic grip. Six months in qvevri clay — ancient method, modern revelation. The wine that started the orange wave.

  • Ramato "Vino del Borgo" Radikon · Friuli Colli Orientali · 2018

    $128

    Pinot Grigio

    Copper-amber. Dried orange peel, walnut skin, wild honey, earthy depth. Complex and evolving over hours in the glass. Not for the fainthearted.

  • Movia Lunar Movia · Brda · 2020

    $18 / $84

    Ribolla Gialla

    Burnished gold. Saffron, dried mango, dried rose, gentle tannic texture. Lunar refers to the biodynamic calendar used during vinification. A rare encounter.
