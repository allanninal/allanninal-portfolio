Design System

Every token this template uses, and what it is for.

Re-skinning this template means changing the values in the :root block of src/styles/global.css and nothing else. This page renders whatever is in there right now, so it stays true after you have changed it.

Colour

Two constraints decided this palette and neither was stylistic. The warm band, the greens and the blues are all taken by earlier days. And this template sits directly next to day 75 in the gallery grid, which is a cool drafting grey with an indigo mark on it — so the neighbour has to be warm, domestic and unmistakably a house. That leaves the magenta-plum band, which is right for a reason beyond availability: the kitchen is the one room where a saturated cabinet colour is normal. Warm rose plaster carrying a purple mark is not confusable at thumbnail size with warm yellow paper carrying an olive mark, or with cool grey carrying indigo. Every ratio below was computed with tools/a11y/contrast.py, not eyeballed.

Ground

  • --color-bg Page ground — plaster, hue 27°
  • --color-bg-alt Alternating band and record cards — chalk
  • --color-surface Cards, forms and the week strips — sheet white
  • --color-border Hairlines — decorative only, 1.41:1
  • --color-border-strong Interactive borders 3.41:1 AA

Ink

  • --color-text Body and headings — ink 16.01:1 AAA
  • --color-text-faint Labels and eyebrows 9.27:1 AAA
  • --color-text-muted Secondary prose — warm grey 5.84:1 AA

Accent

  • --color-accent Buttons, rules and text — damson 6.49:1 AA
  • --color-accent-hover Pressed state 10.42:1 AAA
  • --color-accent-text Accent-coloured text 5.71:1 AA
  • --color-booking Booking bar ground 7.21:1 AAA
  • --color-required Required-field mark only 7.21:1 AAA

Record flags

  • --color-flag-over Record: ran past the promise 8.98:1 AAA
  • --color-flag-under Record: finished early 7.48:1 AAA
  • --color-flag-even Record: on the promised day 10.31:1 AAA

Week strip

  • --color-strip-full Week strip — solid block 7.21:1 AAA
  • --color-strip-part Week strip — hatched block 3.73:1 AA (graphical)
  • --color-strip-none Week strip — outline only 3.79:1 AA (graphical)

Dark sections

  • --color-dark-bg Hero, living-here band, footer
  • --color-dark-surface Cards inside dark bands
  • --color-dark-text Text on dark 16.01:1 AAA
  • --color-dark-faint Maker strip and callouts 11.39:1 AAA
  • --color-dark-muted Secondary on dark 8.44:1 AAA
  • --color-dark-accent Accent on dark — damson light 9.32:1 AAA

Two tokens carry the same idea on two grounds, which is the correct answer rather than a duplication: no single value clears 4.5:1 against both plaster and ink, so the accent is --color-accent on light and --color-dark-accent on dark. The maker strip and the download bar are written as literal hex rather than as var() fallback chains, because a fallback chain that resolves to currentColor is not a fix — it is a silent no-op that changes nothing and looks like a repair in the diff.

The three record flags are deliberately not a traffic light. Two of the six records on the home page ran past what was promised and that is the point of the section, so "ran over" is set at the same weight and the same legibility as "finished early". A flag that shouts would turn a record back into an advert.

The week strip is the one place in this template where a block of colour carries meaning, and it is deliberately not carried by colour. Solid, hatched and outline are three different shapes, every block also renders a screen-reader reading of what it means on its own row, and the strip is drawn on white rather than chalk because the mid fill measures 3.73:1 on white and only 2.95:1 on chalk.

Type

Faustina for display, Hanken Grotesk for body, and nothing else. The serif is a gallery decision before it is a craft one: days 74 and 75 both broke to grotesks and a third consecutive grotesk would make three adjacent thumbnails read as one system. Faustina is a compact humanist serif with a large x-height drawn for tight settings, which is the practical requirement too — the headlines here are long and domestic and a wide display serif breaks them over four lines on a phone. Hanken Grotesk is a warm geometric-humanist sans with a generous x-height, and the displacement records are why it wins: six labelled lines per project at small sizes, full of dates and day counts, sitting next to a week strip whose column labels are tiny.

No third family, and specifically no monospace. Day 75 loaded a mono for its ledger columns; repeating a mono figure column here would echo the neighbour in exactly the wrong place, and the numbers on this page are day counts inside prose lines rather than columns that must align. Where figures do stack — the week strip — the .figures class sets font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums on Hanken Grotesk, which is harmless where unsupported.

    THE MIDDLE, NOT THE AFTER

Interaction states

Every interactive element carries a themed hover and a visible keyboard focus ring. Tab through the row below rather than clicking it — the focus ring is the part most templates leave as the browser default, and it is the one a keyboard user depends on. Nothing here is muted with opacity: opacity composites text toward its background, which is exactly what contrast measures.

Decorative marks

Every decorative mark in this template is an inline SVG, and none of them is a character. Day 74 shipped a Geometric Shapes glyph with a variation selector and it rendered as a tofu box on machines whose fonts did not cover it; five sibling templates were converted to inline SVG for the same reason. Day 75 still set the Pro download bar's padlock as an emoji codepoint, which is the same bet with a different die; here it is drawn. A glyph is a font-coverage bet, an SVG is not.

Spacing and radii

Sections are separated by --section-gap. Everything uses a 4px radius and nothing uses a pill, because a cabinet door has square corners and a page about a room should not look like an app. Nothing else invents a value.

  • 0.5rem tight stacks
  • 1rem inside cards
  • 1.5rem between cards
  • --section-gap between sections
