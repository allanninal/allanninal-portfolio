Reference

Design system

Every ratio was computed with tools/a11y/contrast.py against all three grounds. This page is noindex: it documents the template for whoever builds with it.

The credit column is the portfolio

No album artwork anywhere. A wall of covers tells a visitor who somebody has been in a room with; the right-hand column tells them what was done once they were there, which is the only part of a discography that matters to anyone hiring. The four tallies above the table are counted from it, so an edit to one release cannot leave a headline lying.

A role that was not done is absent, not red

Four jobs, but only one chip colour. A credit either applies to a record or it does not, and the absence of a chip says that better than a second colour would — there is no such thing as a negative credit. The chips are text in the markup, so the roles read correctly with CSS off and to a screen reader.

Photographs again, after three without

Days 108, 109 and 110 each had a specific reason for having no images: a fictional game's screenshots, a fictional game's store captures, and headshots of nine people who do not exist. None of those applies to a producer, whose room is a real kind of place, so the desk and the room are here — one photograph per craft, captioned as the craft rather than as a claim.

The points section ends at $2.85

The final line is styled heavier and takes the one non-accent colour on the page, because the whole section exists to arrive at a number small enough that stating it plainly is the argument. A worked list rather than a claim: five lines, one operation each.
