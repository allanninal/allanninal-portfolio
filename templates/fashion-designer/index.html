How to read this line sheet

Six terms, defined once, used consistently in every table below.

MOQ
Minimum order quantity — the smallest number of units you can order per colourway, per style. Below the table, that number is 2 or 3 depending on the style.
Colourway
A style’s available colours for a given season. Two blazers in the same cut but different colourways are the same style number, not two styles.
Size run
The full span of sizes a style ships in — 00 through 16 across this line sheet. A buyer orders the MOQ spread across the run, not the MOQ in one size.
Delivery window
The season slot a style ships in. This line sheet has two: Delivery 1 and Delivery 2, each with its own order-cutoff date and ship date.
Pre-book vs. at-once
A pre-book order is for goods still in production, placed against a future delivery window. An at-once order ships from stock already on hand. Every style below is a pre-book; reorders on the two carryover bestsellers (the Bellwether Blazer, the Cortlandt Trouser) ship at-once from Delivery 1 stock.
Keystone
The standard 2.0–2.2× markup a retailer applies to a wholesale price to set their own shelf price. Adeyemi Studio publishes both numbers on this line sheet, computed from one keystone ratio — see the line sheet below.

Why deadstock, specifically

Fabric & sourcing

The deadstock story

Deadstock is mill overstock — fabric a larger house ordered and didn't use, or a run that came in short of a minimum somewhere else. Every yard on this line sheet is deadstock on purpose: it doesn't require a custom minimum yardage, which is exactly the constraint a small label kept small by choice runs into everywhere else. It isn't a sustainability badge stapled onto the copy — it's the reason a label this size can run eight styles a season instead of two.

Garment District, on purpose

Studio & making

The studio

Amara Adeyemi designs, drapes and grades every pattern from a fourth-floor studio at 250 W 39th St, Suite 4B, New York — the Garment District, a short walk from most of the contractors and jobbers this line depends on.

The CMT contractor

Cutting, making and trimming is done by a five-person CMT shop two blocks north — not offshore, and not because offshore is wrong, but because a label this size can't yet meet most offshore factories' minimums without buying more fabric than a deadstock supply chain can promise twice.

What's cut where

Amara cuts and fits the first sample of every style herself, in-house. Grading across the full 00–16 run, cutting production yardage, and construction all happen at the CMT shop, from the same pattern.

FAQ

Questions

Do you run sample sales?

Twice a year, timed to when each season’s samples come back from market appointments — announced by email to the wholesale list and on Amara’s LinkedIn, not a standing public sale.

Can I cancel or change a PO after it’s placed?

Before the delivery window’s order-cutoff date, yes — email or LinkedIn message Amara directly. After cutoff, goods are already cut against your quantities, so changes are limited to swapping colourways within the same style if fabric allows.

What’s the minimum to reorder a style already in my store?

$350 wholesale, same as any reorder — see Wholesale terms above. Reorders on the two carryover styles ship at-once; everything else is next season’s pre-book.
