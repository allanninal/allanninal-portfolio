Our Story

Rooted in care.
Grounded in expertise.

Serenity & Stone was founded in 2012 by a team of licensed therapists who believed that wellness should feel both scientifically grounded and deeply human. We opened our doors in Brooklyn with six treatment rooms, a commitment to using only non-toxic, sustainably sourced products, and a single guiding principle: every guest leaves feeling more themselves.

Over twelve years, we have grown to seven suites, two private infrared sauna rooms, and a team of fifteen therapists and estheticians — each with a minimum of five years' clinical training. Our protocols are updated annually in partnership with dermatologists and physiotherapists who share our philosophy.

We are proud members of the International Spa Association and hold A+ hygiene certification from the NYC Department of Health.
