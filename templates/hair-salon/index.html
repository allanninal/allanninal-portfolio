Our Story

A Space Built for Beautiful Hair

Maison Lumière was founded in 2013 by Isabelle March with a single belief: that a great haircut should feel like a reset — a small, quiet luxury in the middle of the week that changes how you carry yourself for the next six weeks.

What started as a two-chair studio in Chelsea has grown into a four-stylist atelier. We've never pursued size. We've pursued skill. Each of our stylists spent years training at named institutions and working in demanding environments before joining us — which means you're always in experienced hands.

Our approach is editorial without being precious. We take technical colour and cutting seriously, but we also listen carefully enough to know when what you really want is something low-maintenance that grows out beautifully.
