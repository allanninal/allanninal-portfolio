What a print outlives

Pigment disclosure

Wilhelm Imaging Research testing rates a correctly produced archival pigment print on cotton rag at 100–200+ years of display life. Whether the original lasts that long depends on which pigments are in it, rated ASTM I (excellent) through IV/V (poor). Genuine Alizarin Crimson (PR83) is rated ASTM III–IV and is "completely fugitive in washes" — Winsor & Newton replaced it with a lightfast Permanent Alizarin Crimson hue (ASTM I) in 1994. Genuine Sap Green is the same story: historically "highly fugitive," which is why almost every modern tube sold as Sap Green today is actually a lightfast hue mixture, not this pigment. Teresa paints with the genuine article in both cases — the colour on this page is the same colour under discussion below.

Ground to varnish

How it's made

  1. Ground Linen sized and primed, or a cradled panel gessoed and sanded, so the surface is even before the first mark.
  2. Block-in A thin, lean wash of turpentine and paint establishes composition and value — nothing here is thick yet.
  3. Working layers Fat-over-lean passes build form over days or weeks, thickest in the passages that will read as texture from across a room.
  4. Touch-dry The surface is dry to the finger within days to about two weeks, depending on thickness. This is not cured — only dry to the touch.
  5. Minimum cure The oxidative cure runs through the full paint film, not just the surface: 2 months for thin, lean layers; 6 months for standard alla-prima work; up to 2 years for thick impasto.
  6. Varnish A final protective coat is applied only after the minimum cure for that painting’s build — never before, whatever a listing might say.

How a print is made

  1. Digital capture The finished original is scanned or photographed under D65-balanced light and colour-matched by eye against the panel or canvas.
  2. Proof One proof print is checked against the original in daylight before an edition is approved — the print is judged against the object, not a screen.
  3. Archival pigment print 11-colour archival pigment inkjet on 310gsm cotton rag, acid-free, 100% cotton fibre.
  4. Ship Rolled in a rigid tube for 12 × 16 in and larger; flat-packed for 8 × 10 in. Ready to frame.
