Design system

Every ratio was computed with tools/a11y/contrast.py, not eyeballed. This page is noindex: it documents the template for whoever builds with it.

Sage, not pastel

A nursery palette would undercut the only thing this page is trying to say. Deep sage at hue 141° reads calm without reading soft, which is the register a page about safety needs.

The method is a word, never a colour

Every pose is marked Composite, Supported or Not attempted in text. The fill and the border style repeat that word. On a page whose whole purpose is disclosure, encoding the disclosure in colour alone would be the worst possible place to do it.

The counts are derived

"Three of these are composites" is counted from the pose list, and a test recounts it from the rendered markers. A page that miscounted its own disclosures would be worse than one that made none.
