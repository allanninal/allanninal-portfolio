Who reads it

Marginalia is a weekly newsletter about used bookstores, ephemeral printed objects, and the small histories that live in the margins. Sent every Sunday since 2022 — sometimes 600 words, sometimes 1,200, never an unread.

  • Working writers, editors, and journalists
  • Rare-book dealers, librarians, and archivists
  • Collectors of print ephemera and book-history buffs
  • Designers and typographers who care about the object, not just the text

What a placement includes

  • A single classified-style placement in one Sunday issue — written in the voice of the letter, never a banner.
  • A line in the archive copy, which keeps earning clicks long after send.
  • One sponsor per issue. No ad network, no retargeting, no tracking pixels.
  • A short, honest note from the author about why the product fits the audience.
