Why band width changes the size

A wide band contacts more of the knuckle on the way over it than a thin one does at the exact same finger circumference, so it needs more clearance to pass comfortably.

Band widthTypical adjustment
1.5–2 mm True to a standard fitting — no adjustment.
4–5 mm Usually a quarter size up from a thin band on the same finger.
6–8 mm Usually a half size up — the wider face has to clear the same knuckle.
9 mm+ A half to full size up, and worth trying on in person before ordering.

Settings that cannot be resized

  • Eternity bands — stones set all the way around leave no bare metal to stretch or cut.
  • Channel-set bands — stones sit inside a continuous metal channel that can’t be safely opened.
  • Some tension settings — the stone is held by the band’s own spring tension, which a resize would loosen or break.
