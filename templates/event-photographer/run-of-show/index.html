Run of Show.

The demo marks one event's run of show. This is the blank instrument — six fields to fill in, 17 shots that follow from them, and the same-night workflow that makes a before-midnight delivery possible rather than aspirational.

Six fields per row

Time
24-hour, to five minutes
Not "morning". A camera position is a decision about a particular minute.
What happens
One line, plain
"Award 3 of 7, sponsor presents" is useful. "Ceremony" is not.
Where
Room and stage side
A photographer walking into the wrong room has missed it.
Who
Names, not titles
And a face. Titles cannot be recognised across a dark auditorium.
Must-have?
Yes / no
Marks which slots a camera may not leave. Usually three or four in a day.
Clashes with
Another row, or blank
The only field that tells a photographer whether one camera is enough.

The shot list

Room

  • ☐Empty and branded
  • ☐Filling, from the back
  • ☐At capacity, from high if there is a balcony

Stage

  • ☐Each speaker, wide
  • ☐Each speaker, tight
  • ☐Speaker with the screen legible behind

Audience

  • ☐Reacting during the key moment
  • ☐Listening, mid-row
  • ☐Taking notes or photographing the screen

People

  • ☐Every named must-have, at least twice
  • ☐Two-person conversations at the break
  • ☐Hands, badges, programmes

Awards

  • ☐The handshake, once
  • ☐The staged portrait afterwards
  • ☐The trophy, alone, on a clean surface

Sponsors

  • ☐Each stand with a person at it
  • ☐Branding in a natural context, not photographed as a logo

Same-night workflow

  1. During the day Cull on the back of the camera between sessions Flagging as you go turns a two-hour evening edit into forty minutes.
  2. On leaving Two card copies before the car moves The only irreplaceable files you will ever handle. Nothing else on this page matters if this step is skipped.
  3. +30 min Ingest, select 20–30, nothing clever The social team needs correct and fast, not graded. Perfection tomorrow is worth less than adequate tonight.
  4. +90 min Exposure, white balance, crop, send A shared link, sized for social, named by moment rather than by frame number.
  5. Next morning The full working set By the time the office opens, which is when the press release goes.

Five things that go wrong without it

  1. 1 No run of show, so the photographer follows the day instead of anticipating it.
  2. 2 Must-have people identified by title rather than by face.
  3. 3 A clash nobody flagged, discovered at the moment both things happen.
  4. 4 The award portraits not staged, because nobody asked and the winners left.
  5. 5 Delivery promised for "next week", by which time the event has stopped being news.

Timings assume a single-venue event within an hour of the studio. Multi-venue days need their own conversation and usually a second photographer.
