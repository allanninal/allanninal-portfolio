The green means someone is speaking

--color-accent is used for exactly one thing on the demo: words a sitter said. The wall at the top, and the pull-quote in each sitting. Not navigation, not buttons, not emphasis generally. Hue 115° also happens to be the emptiest bucket in the library — one template across the 91 before this, and that one is a muted 23%-saturation olive at 106° rather than this 44%.

The account sits on the picture

Above 900px each sitting is a 12-column grid where the image spans 1–7 and the account spans 7–12, so they overlap by a column. Below that the overlap is removed entirely rather than scaled down — a quote on top of a face is only legible when there is room for it.
