Reference

Design system

Every ratio was computed with tools/a11y/contrast.py against all three grounds. This page is noindex: it documents the template for whoever builds with it.

The disclaimer is a token, not a paragraph

One constant in config.ts supplies the notice in the hero, the notice on the Pro page, and the disclaimer property in the structured data. schema.org has that property on Service and it is the single most important field on this page: a machine-readable service description about trademarks that reads as legal advice is precisely the failure the page is written against. The reader and the parser get the same sentence because it is the same string.

No offer catalogue for somebody else’s fees

The cost table quotes USPTO filing fees, approximate and per class. They are not this business’s prices, so they are not emitted as offers — a rich result showing “$350” against this studio would be false in a way nobody would ever notice. The Nice classes are emitted as a plain ItemList instead, which asserts only that they were considered.

Strength is a word, not a bar

The distinctiveness spectrum runs generic to fanciful and the obvious design is a five-step bar. It is not drawn as one, because a bar implies a measurable scale and trademark distinctiveness is a set of categories a court argues about. Printing “Nearly unownable” is both more honest and more useful than a bar at 20%.

Three outcome colours, three kinds of thing

Filed, Not yet, No. Day 123 carried its verdicts on two signals because colour was reserved for the client; here the three outcomes are genuinely different in kind rather than three points on one scale, so they get three hues — after the word and the glyph, as always. --v-later is deliberately the plain muted text colour: not filing yet is not a result, it is the absence of one.

Body is the system stack. A sturdy serif and a condensed sans, which is roughly what a register entry looks like and is not an accident. Neither face appears in days 1–123.
