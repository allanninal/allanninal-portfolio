## Alt — slightly longer version (LinkedIn, if you want more body)

Just drew up the 8th system in my "small cloud on AWS" catalogue — a review responder.

What it does:
→ Watches every new review across Google Business Profile, your Facebook page, and Yelp
→ Drafts replies in YOUR voice using YOUR own policies (refund window, tone, signature)
→ Cites every factual claim — no inventing prices, dates, or staff names
→ Auto-posts the obvious thanks-yous; one-tap drafts for everything else
→ 1-star and safety language (food poisoning, lawyer, harassment) goes straight to a human, full transcript attached

Architecture above. 7 posts, one diagram each, engineering reference at the end. Designed to run for a coffee a month at SMB review volume.
