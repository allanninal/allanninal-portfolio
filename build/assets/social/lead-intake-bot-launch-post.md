## Alt — slightly longer version (LinkedIn, if you want more body)

Just drew up the 9th system in my "small cloud on AWS" catalogue — a lead intake bot.

What it does:
→ Catches every new lead from your website forms, Meta Lead Ads, Google Ads, and the shared sales inbox
→ Qualifies each one against YOUR ICP and YOUR pricing — three small extractors read intent, urgency, and fit signals
→ Picks one of four moves per lead: hot route, warm follow-up, nurture, reject
→ Hot leads ping the on-call rep in Slack within seconds with a draft reply already written from your pricing doc — they tap claim, edit a sentence, hit send
→ Cold and off-ICP leads never ping the team — they're tagged in the CRM and dropped in nurture, archived with a reason
→ The composer can't invent a discount, an SLA, or an availability promise — citation required, four guardrails

Architecture above. 7 posts, one diagram each, engineering reference at the end. Designed to run for a coffee a month at SMB lead volume.
