Philippine Electricity Analysis

The most coal-dependent grid in Southeast Asia, and the bill that follows

Twenty-six years of generation mix from Ember, set against Southeast Asian peers, joined to Meralco's own monthly residential rate advisories. Coal supplied 58.7% of Philippine electricity in 2025 — more than double its 2005 share, and the highest of any major economy in the region.

Key Takeaways

The Philippines burns more coal for its electricity than any other major economy in Southeast Asia, and the share has more than doubled since 2005. That is the structural reason the bill is high and moves with the peso.

  • Coal supplied 58.7% of generation in 2025, against 27.0% in 2005. It peaked at 63.9% in 2024.
  • Thailand runs at 17.8% coal, Singapore at 0.9%. The Philippine share is more than three times Thailand's.
  • Meralco's all-in residential rate went from P12.29/kWh in March 2025 to P14.78 in August 2026 — about 20% in eighteen months.
  • Solar reached 2.4% of generation in 2025. Renewables other than hydro have been roughly flat as a share for a decade while coal grew.
  • Because the coal is imported, the fuel cost tracks world prices and the exchange rate rather than domestic supply.

What this does and does not prove

A coal-heavy mix explains why the bill is exposed to world coal prices and the peso. It is not by itself a full account of the retail rate, which also carries transmission, distribution, taxes and subsidies this dataset does not break out.

Methodology

How This Was Built

Two sources, one a clean bulk download and one scraped out of press advisories.

📊

Ember yearly electricity

Free, keyless bulk CSV covering 224 areas. 208 Philippine rows across 2000-2025 and 226 coal-share rows for the regional comparison.

⚠️

Ember calls it "The Philippines"

Not "Philippines". Filtering on the obvious string returns zero rows and an empty chart rather than an error, so a check asserts the country is still present in the download.

⚡

Meralco advisories by URL pattern

The rates archive is JavaScript-rendered; robots.txt itself returns the app shell. Advisories are reached through five slug shapes, and coverage is recorded per month attempted.

🔍

The generation charge trap

Each advisory quotes several figures per kWh. The generation charge is phrased exactly like the all-in rate but is roughly half of it. The first parser captured it by mistake; extraction is now anchored to the overall-rate sentence.

🧪

Two advisories per month, cross-checked

Each advisory states both the current and prior month, so consecutive advisories overlap. Where they disagreed the run reported it — which is how the generation-charge error was found, not by reading the numbers.

📌

What is not claimed

This page does not compare retail tariffs across countries. It compares generation mix, which is well sourced, and reports one utility's rate. Cross-country tariff comparison needs a price source this project does not have.
