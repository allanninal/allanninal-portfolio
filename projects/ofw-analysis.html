Key Takeaways

2.19 million OFWs sent PHP 262 billion in remittances in 2024, with women comprising 57.2% of the overseas workforce and Saudi Arabia as the top destination.

  • OFW population grew 1.5% to 2.19 million, with total remittances up 9.9% to PHP 262 billion.
  • Female OFWs grew 4.4% while male OFWs declined 2.2%, reinforcing the feminization of overseas labor.
  • Saudi Arabia hosts 21.9% of all OFWs; the Middle East collectively accounts for ~49% of deployments.
  • Banks handle 61% of cash remittances, with 88.4% of OFWs sending money home averaging PHP 129K per worker.

Occupation Distribution

Four in every 10 OFWs work in elementary occupations

🧹 Elementary Occupations

Gender Patterns by Destination

Different destinations attract different gender profiles

📊 Male vs Female OFW Distribution by Country

👩 Female-Dominated Destinations

Hong Kong (10.7% F vs 0.5% M) and Kuwait (10.1% F vs 1.3% M) employ predominantly female domestic workers. Saudi Arabia also has more female OFWs (24.7% vs 18.2%).

👨 Male-Dominated Destinations

Japan (5.8% M vs 1.6% F) employs more male workers in manufacturing and construction. Australia also has higher male representation.

Key Findings & Insights

Summary of critical insights from the 2024 OFW Survey

📈 Growth Trends

  • OFW population grew 1.5% to 2.19 million
  • Total remittances increased 9.9% to PHP 262 billion
  • Female OFWs grew 4.4% while male declined 2.2%
  • Average remittance per OFW up 4.7% to PHP 129K

📊 Demographic Patterns

  • Women dominate at 57.2% of OFW workforce
  • Mature workers (45+) are the largest age group
  • CALABARZON provides 1 in 5 OFWs
  • Luzon contributes 63% of all OFWs

🌏 Destination Insights

  • Asia hosts 74.5% of all OFWs
  • Saudi Arabia is #1 destination (21.9%)
  • Middle East dominates with ~49% of OFWs
  • Hong Kong/Kuwait almost exclusively female

💰 Remittance Insights

  • Banks handle 61% of cash remittances
  • OFWs in Americas send highest per-capita
  • 88.4% of OFWs send cash remittances
  • Cash sent grew 14.5%, brought home fell 9.3%

Data Source & Methodology

This analysis uses official data from the Philippine Statistics Authority's Survey on Overseas Filipinos (SOF) 2024, released December 2025.

  • Source: Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
  • Survey Period: April to September 2024
  • Coverage: Overseas Filipinos who departed within last 5 years and worked abroad in past 6 months
  • Release Date: December 16, 2025
  • Reference: PSA 2025-437
