Stripe fix guides

Stripe fails quietly. A webhook endpoint stops delivering and the payments still succeed, so revenue looks normal while everything that should happen after a payment silently stops. Each note here explains one such problem and gives you a script that finds it through the API, in Python and in Node.js, with tests.

Read-only keyPython and Node.jsTests included

Why these scripts never write

Every other section on this site ships scripts that can repair what they find. These do not, on purpose. A script here holds a credential to a live payments account, where a bug does not cost you a stale cache — it cancels a subscription, refunds a charge, or disables a connected account's payouts.

So they read, they tell you exactly what is wrong, and they print the repair: the endpoint, the parameters, the object id. You run it. Give them a restricted key with read access only and the worst case if one leaks is that somebody learns your webhook URLs.

A webhook endpoint sits disabled after days of retries

Events flowed for months, then stopped on one date with nothing in the logs. Stripe disabled the endpoint and no longer retries it.
StripeDiagnostic

Undelivered events are aging out of the 30-day window

An outage found three weeks late. The fix takes minutes; the replay is the problem, because the oldest missed events are about to leave the API.
StripeDiagnostic

An endpoint subscribes to every event and floods the handler

The webhook route receives dozens of types it has no branch for, and times out at month-end. enabled_events is a wildcard, not a list.
StripeDiagnostic

Two endpoints share one URL, so every event is handled twice

Duplicate orders and double fulfilment emails, not reproducible locally. Two enabled endpoints point at the same URL and both signatures verify.
StripeDiagnostic

Payout.failed is unsubscribed so failures go unseen for days

Money stops arriving in the bank account and nothing alerts. payout.paid is subscribed, payout.failed is not, and the external account is disabled.
StripeDiagnostic

Incomplete subscriptions die silently after 23 hours

A subscription that never gets its first invoice paid sits in incomplete for exactly 23 hours, then expires and cannot be revived.
StripeDiagnostic

Active subscriptions with nothing to charge on renewal

Some subscriptions read active but have no payment method in any of the four places Stripe looks. Every renewal fails and no retry is ever scheduled.
StripeDiagnostic

Past_due subscriptions keep their access forever

Failed renewals move a subscription to past_due and leave it there. The app only checks for canceled, so the customer keeps everything and pays nothing.
StripeDiagnostic

Trials ending in days with no card on file

A cohort of trialing subscriptions has no payment method. On the trial end date they all fail at once, and the default behaviour is silent dunning.
StripeDiagnostic

3DS handoff breaks and requires_action intents pile up

PaymentIntents freeze at requires_action when the client never finishes the authentication handoff. No error is raised and no money moves.
StripeDiagnostic

PaymentIntents sit in requires_payment_method for weeks

Intents created on page load and never confirmed pile up forever. Checkout starts look healthy while payment volume quietly does not match them.
StripeDiagnostic

Radar blocks payments and nobody reads the block reasons

Blocked charges never reach the issuer, so they leave no decline code. A rule written years ago can quietly eat good revenue for months.
StripeDiagnostic

Refunds sit failed or requires_action and nobody notices

A refund is not final when created. It can fail days later, and if nothing listens the money leaves your balance and reaches no one.
StripeDiagnostic

A connected account sits with charges_enabled false

A seller's checkout stops working and the platform dashboard looks fine, because the account that broke is not the one you are looking at.
StripeDiagnostic

Requirements.past_due has already disabled the payouts

A monitor that counts currently_due cannot tell a warning from a broken account, because past_due is a subset of the list it is counting.
StripeDiagnostic

Payouts fail with account_closed and nobody is watching

Money leaves your Stripe balance, comes back days later, and the recipient was never paid. The failure_code says which fix applies, if anything reads it.
StripeDiagnostic

A connected account has no external account to pay out to

A seller's balance climbs for months with no failed payouts, because no payout was ever attempted. There is nowhere for the money to go.
StripeDiagnostic

Disputes are hours from due_by with no evidence attached

Nothing chases a dispute deadline. Read evidence_details.due_by against now and find the ones about to be lost by default rather than on merit.
StripeDiagnostic

Disputes closed as lost were never actually contested

A wall of lost disputes hides two different failures. A lost dispute with submission_count 0 was forfeited by the deadline, not decided against you.
StripeDiagnostic

Checkout Sessions carry no ID that maps back to your order

A payment lands and nothing on it points at your database. client_reference_id and metadata both default to empty, and nothing warns you.
StripeDiagnostic

Duplicate customers share an email and split billing

Stripe does not enforce email uniqueness on Customers. One person ends up with three records, and their card, subscription and invoices sit on different ones.
